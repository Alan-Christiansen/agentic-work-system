> [!info]- What this file is for
> **Purpose:** Explore future possibilities without adding them to the committed product plan.
> **Developed explorations:** Give a promising idea enough room to examine its potential value, risks, prerequisites, and implications.
> **Conditions worth watching:** Record evidence, technology changes, user demand, business conditions, or completed milestones that could make an exploration worth reconsidering.
> **Keep elsewhere:** Fragments belong in [[_Idea Inbox]]; capabilities under serious consideration for a real release belong in [[Feature Landscape]].
