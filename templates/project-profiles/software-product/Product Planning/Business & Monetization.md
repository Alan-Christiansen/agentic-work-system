> [!info]- What this file is for
> **Purpose:** Explore how the product could create sustainable business value without treating early assumptions as settled business facts.
> **Customer and value exchange:** Identify who uses the product, who might pay, who benefits, and what outcome makes the exchange worthwhile. The customer, user, and beneficiary may be different people or organizations.
> **Monetization options:** Compare plausible models such as free, one-time purchase, subscription, paid upgrade, sponsorship, licensing, or a hybrid. Note why each model might fit, what behavior it encourages, and what tradeoffs it creates.
> **Pricing:** Capture packaging ideas, price hypotheses, the boundary between free and paid value, willingness-to-pay evidence, and questions that must be tested before choosing a price.
> **Costs and constraints:** Track costs that could shape viability, including development, hosting, third-party services, payment fees, platform fees, support, maintenance, compliance, and the operational load of serving customers. Include ethical constraints on how the product should and should not make money.
> **Evidence and open questions:** Distinguish observed demand, comparable products, interviews, experiments, or actual purchasing behavior from guesses. Record the smallest useful validation step for important unknowns.
> **Keep elsewhere:** Company-wide strategy and finances belong in the appropriate business-planning source; product capabilities belong in [[Feature Landscape]]; implementation costs that require code-level analysis belong in the companion repository.
