# Product Requirements
## Problem
Describe the user or customer problem worth solving.

## Intended users
- Name the people who experience the problem.

## Desired outcome
Describe the useful change the product should create.

## Scope
### In
- State the smallest coherent product scope.

### Out
- State tempting but excluded work.

## User needs and use cases
- Describe the important situation and desired result.

## Requirements
- PRD-001: State one observable product requirement.

## Success evidence
- Describe what would show that the product is useful or commercially promising.

## Constraints
- Record business, accessibility, privacy, platform, or timing constraints.

## Open questions
- Keep unresolved product questions visible without treating them as decisions.

## Related locations
- Link the companion repository and relevant research when they exist.
