## Constraints and open decisions
> [!info]- What this file is for
> **Purpose:** Capture product-level technical direction and constraints that affect what can be built or how it can be delivered.
> **Technology stack:** Record selected or seriously considered languages, frameworks, platforms, and foundational services.
> **Platform, hosting, and delivery:** Capture supported devices or browsers, hosting direction, deployment expectations, availability, and distribution constraints.
> **Data, privacy, and security:** Identify what data is collected or stored, where it moves, its sensitivity, and the privacy or security obligations it creates.
> **Integrations and external services:** Record dependencies on APIs, vendors, authentication providers, analytics, payments, or other outside systems.
> **Constraints and open decisions:** Keep feasibility limits, cost or maintenance concerns, and unresolved foundational choices visible without presenting them as accepted architecture.
> **Keep elsewhere:** Detailed architecture, source-code documentation, tests, and implementation history belong in the companion repository.
