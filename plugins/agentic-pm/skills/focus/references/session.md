# Session focus
Use this mode for a bounded work session, an immediate next action, or a stuck state.
## Establish the session
Confirm the selected domain or record, time available, and any material energy, attention, location, tool, dependency, or hard-stop constraint.
## Choose a normal next action
1. Reread the relevant human home and current `xPM/` state. If the request begins from a domain, consider only managed records in that domain.
2. Prefer an action that advances the current primary outcome, unblocks valuable work, or prevents a near-term cost.
3. Reduce the action until its finish condition is observable within the session. When execution does not fit, choose bounded preparation, clarification, maintenance, or recovery.
4. Name what should not receive attention during the session.
## Diagnose stuckness when present
1. Identify the active constraint: unclear outcome, oversized action, missing information, unresolved decision, external dependency, environment friction, fatigue, emotional resistance, or weak strategic value.
2. Ask at most one short diagnostic question when the answer would materially change the move. Otherwise make a transparent, conservative inference.
3. Reduce the constraint to one action that can begin immediately and finish within the available capacity.
4. When recovery is the constraint, recommend an explicit reset or recovery action rather than disguising it as productivity.
5. Preserve the larger task and its meaning; do not rewrite it into another commitment merely to make progress appear easier.
## Deliver
Use a short form: one imperative action, finish condition, likely constraint when relevant, first move, and what can wait.
