# Weekly focus
Use this mode to turn the current direction into a capacity-aware plan for one week or a similar short multi-day horizon.
## Plan
1. Establish the selected domain or record, actual available work time, hard stops, and material energy or attention constraints.
2. Load the relevant managed Areas and Projects through the shared policy's minimal context path. Respect current tasks, deadlines, blockers, dependencies, and accepted decisions.
3. Identify the one outcome that would make the horizon meaningfully successful. Add at most one secondary maintenance outcome when neglect would create a real cost.
4. Break the primary outcome into a small sequence of concrete moves sized to the available capacity. Keep later work visibly later.
5. Include recovery margin rather than filling every available hour. Flag any plan that depends on unresolved approval, estimate, or external event.
## Deliver
State the primary outcome, optional secondary maintenance outcome, first action, rough capacity allocation, displacement, and replanning condition. Do not create a detailed calendar unless the user separately asks for scheduling.
