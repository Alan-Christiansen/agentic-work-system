# Stuck Rescue

Read [the shared operating policy](../../references/operating-policy.md) completely before acting.

## Rescue

1. Identify the work the user is trying to move and the smallest relevant source of truth. Do not load the whole portfolio unless competing commitments are causing the stuck state.
2. Test the likely constraint: unclear outcome, oversized action, missing information, unresolved decision, external dependency, environment friction, fatigue, emotional resistance, or weak strategic value.
3. Ask at most one short diagnostic question when the answer would materially change the move. Otherwise make a transparent, conservative inference.
4. Reduce the obstacle to one action that can be started immediately and finished within the available capacity. When recovery is the constraint, recommend an explicit recovery or reset action rather than disguising it as productivity.
5. Preserve the larger task and its meaning; do not rewrite it into a different commitment merely to make progress appear easier.

Do not change task state or canonical next actions unless the user asks to save the result. Do not perform the larger represented task unless separately requested.

## Deliver

Use a short form: likely constraint, one next move, finish condition, and what can wait.
