# Choose Next Action

Read [the shared operating policy](../../references/operating-policy.md) completely before acting.

## Choose

1. Establish the selected domain or explicitly selected Area or Project, time available, and any material energy, attention, location, tool, or hard-stop constraint.
2. Reread the relevant human home and current xPM state. If the request starts from a domain, consider only managed records in that domain.
3. Prefer an action that advances the current primary outcome, unblocks valuable work, or prevents a near-term cost. Avoid switching merely because another task is easier or newer.
4. Reduce the action until its finish condition is observable within the session. When no meaningful execution fits, choose bounded preparation, clarification, or maintenance.
5. Name what should not receive attention during this session.

Return a recommendation only. Do not mark a task in progress, change `next_action`, or perform the action unless the user explicitly requests that mutation or execution.

## Deliver

Lead with one action in imperative form, then give its finish condition, why it fits now, the first physical or digital move, and the work being deferred.
